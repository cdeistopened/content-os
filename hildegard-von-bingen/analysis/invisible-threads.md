# Invisible Threads in Hildegard von Bingen

> A thematic analysis across the Symphonia hymns, applying the [Invisible Threads](../../skills/writing/invisible-threads/SKILL.md) framework to discover non-obvious connections in Hildegard's corpus.

## The Approach

The Invisible Threads skill finds recurring ideas that operate *below* the stated subject of a text. Applied to Hildegard's hymns, it means looking past what the hymns are *about* (praising God, honoring Mary, invoking the Holy Spirit) and asking what they are *doing* at the level of individual word choices, verb systems, image clusters, and structural moves.

Working from seven hymns in the [Selected Hymns](../hymns-and-sequences/selected-hymns.md) collection, six threads emerged. Each is explored in its own document.

## The Threads

1. **[The Sweat Theology](thread-01-sweat-theology.md)** — The verb *sudare* ("to sweat") appears in three unrelated hymns across three unrelated subjects. It turns out to be Hildegard's master verb for how the divine enters the material world.

2. **[The Garment System](thread-02-garment-system.md)** — *forthcoming* — Clothing, dressing, laundering, girding. The Incarnation as God getting dressed. Salvation as textile work.

3. **[Sound as Ontology](thread-03-sound-as-ontology.md)** — *forthcoming* — The Trinity *is* sound. Christ is the "first voice." The Devil can't sing. Music is not praise — it's the substance of reality.

4. **[The Eve-Mary Inversion Engine](thread-04-eve-mary-inversion.md)** — *forthcoming* — Seven stanzas of abundance, then one line: *Hec omnia Eva contempsit.* How asymmetric proportion performs theology.

5. **[Viriditas as Connective Tissue](thread-05-viriditas.md)** — *forthcoming* — One word doing the work of systematic theology, connecting body, cosmos, God, and moral life each time it appears.

6. **[Synesthesia as Method](thread-06-synesthesia.md)** — *forthcoming* — Heat becomes fragrance, light becomes taste, green becomes moral. Not decoration — epistemology.

## Extraction Prompt

For running the Invisible Threads pipeline computationally on the full 77-piece Symphonia corpus:

```
You are an insight extractor analyzing the hymns, sequences, antiphons,
and theological writings of Hildegard von Bingen (1098-1179).

Your job is to identify genuinely insightful patterns — linguistic moves,
theological arguments, image systems, or structural techniques that reveal
how Hildegard constructs meaning across her body of work.

## What IS an insight (high bar):
An insight must be SPECIFIC + NON-OBVIOUS + ILLUMINATING.

EXAMPLES:
- "Hildegard uses 'sudare' (to sweat) for both the sun's heat entering Mary
  and reason 'sweating' in golden works — the same verb links incarnation to
  intellect, making theology a bodily labor"
- "The refrain 'Et sic indumenta ipsius / a magno dolore / abstersa sunt'
  treats Christ's body as a garment that can be laundered — salvation as
  textile work, a domestic image from a woman's daily life"
- "In O virtus Sapientiae, Wisdom's three wings fly up, sweat down, and
  spread everywhere — a spatial Trinity that maps Father/Son/Spirit onto
  vertical/gravitational/omnipresent axes"

NOT INSIGHTS:
- "Hildegard praises the Virgin Mary" — Too obvious
- "The hymns are beautiful" — Not specific
- "She was a medieval mystic" — Just a fact

CATEGORIES: embodiment, synesthesia, viriditas-network, textile-metaphor,
spatial-theology, sensory-theology, feminine-divine, inversion-rhetoric,
musical-structure, latin-wordplay, cosmological-scale, eve-mary-axis,
liturgical-function, domestic-imagery, botanical-theology, other
```
