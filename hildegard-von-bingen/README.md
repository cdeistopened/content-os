# Hildegard von Bingen: Collected Writings

> The writings, hymns, visions, letters, and philosophy of Hildegard von Bingen (1098–1179), converted to Markdown from public domain sources.

## About This Collection

Hildegard von Bingen was a German Benedictine abbess, writer, composer, philosopher, Christian mystic, visionary, and polymath. Active in the 12th century, she is one of the best-known composers of sacred monody and the most recorded medieval composer. She was canonized in 2012 and named a Doctor of the Church by Pope Benedict XVI.

Her original Latin texts are in the public domain. This collection gathers English translations and Latin originals from various scholarly and public sources, organized for reference and study.
