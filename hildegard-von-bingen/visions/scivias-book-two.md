# Scivias — Book Two: The Redeemer

Book Two consists of seven visions dealing with salvation through Jesus Christ, the Church, and the sacraments.

## Vision 1: The Redeemer

> Then I saw a bright light, and in this light the figure of a man the color of sapphire, which was all blazing with a gentle glowing fire. And that bright light bathed the whole of the glowing fire, and the glowing fire bathed the bright light; and the bright light and the glowing fire poured over the whole human figure, so that the three were one light in one power of potential.

### Interpretation

This vision presents the mystery of the Incarnation. The sapphire figure represents the Son of God clothed in humanity, the bright light is the Father, and the glowing fire is the Holy Spirit. The three interpenetrating lights express the unity of the Trinity.

---

## Vision 2: The Trinity

> And again I heard a voice from Heaven, saying to me: "God, Who made all things by His will through His Word, created humankind in the image and likeness of His Son in body, and in the image of the spirit in soul."

---

## Vision 3: The Church, Bride of Christ

> After this I saw the image of a woman as large as a great city, with a wonderful crown on her head and arms from which a splendor hung like sleeves, shining from Heaven to earth. Her womb was pierced like a net with many openings, with a huge multitude of people running in and out.

### Interpretation

The great woman is the Church — Ecclesia — depicted as both bride and mother. Her net-like womb represents Baptism, through which the faithful enter and leave. This image of the Church as a cosmic female figure is one of Hildegard's most distinctive theological contributions.

---

## Vision 4: Confirmation

> And I saw that this image of the woman had in the region of her breast a splendor like the dawn, in which I perceived a beautiful image of a maiden.

---

## Vision 5: The Three Orders in the Church

> Then I saw three groups of people, two of which stood before the image of the woman near her breast and her womb, and the third stood near the base, beside her feet.

---

## Vision 6: Christ's Sacrifice and the Church

> After this I saw the Son of God hanging on the cross. And the image of the woman whom I had seen before came forward and was placed before the altar of God, so that the blood from His side ran into her mouth.

### Interpretation

The Eucharistic vision: Christ's blood flowing into the mouth of Ecclesia (the Church) represents the sacramental mystery through which the Church is continually nourished and sustained.

---

## Vision 7: The Devil

> And I saw a burning darkness, in which were terrible voices, crying: "Let us go forth to do battle!" And they struck against a mountain of glorious brightness, but could not move it.
