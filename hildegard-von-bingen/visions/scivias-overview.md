# Scivias — Know the Ways of the Lord

> *Sci vias Domini* — "Know the Ways of the Lord"

Completed in 1151, *Scivias* is Hildegard's first and most famous visionary work. It describes 26 religious visions she experienced, structured in three books reflecting the Trinity. Each vision is first described in vivid, often strange imagery, then interpreted theologically in the words of "the voice of the Living Light."

Pope Eugenius III read excerpts at the Synod of Trier (1147–1148) and authorized Hildegard to continue writing her visions.

## Structure

### Book One: The Creator and Creation (6 Visions)
The first book deals with creation, the fall of humanity, and the structure of the universe.

- **Vision 1:** God Enthroned Shows Himself to Hildegard
- **Vision 2:** Creation and the Fall
- **Vision 3:** The Universe and Its Symbolism
- **Vision 4:** Soul and Body
- **Vision 5:** The Synagogue
- **Vision 6:** The Choirs of Angels

### Book Two: The Redeemer (7 Visions)
The second book treats salvation through Jesus Christ, the Church, and the sacraments.

- **Vision 1:** The Redeemer
- **Vision 2:** The Trinity
- **Vision 3:** The Church, Bride of Christ and Mother of the Faithful
- **Vision 4:** Confirmation
- **Vision 5:** The Three Orders in the Church
- **Vision 6:** Christ's Sacrifice and the Church
- **Vision 7:** The Devil

### Book Three: The History of Salvation (13 Visions)
The third and longest book addresses the virtues, the last days, and the new heaven and earth.

- **Vision 1:** God and Man
- **Vision 2:** The Mystical Body
- **Vision 3:** The Tower of the Church
- **Vision 4:** The Pillar of the Word of God
- **Vision 5:** The Zeal of God
- **Vision 6:** The Pillar of the Trinity
- **Vision 7:** The Pillar of the Humanity of the Savior
- **Vision 8:** The Tower of Anticipation of God's Will
- **Vision 9:** The Pillar of True Trinity
- **Vision 10:** The Son of Man
- **Vision 11:** The Last Days and the Fall of the Antichrist
- **Vision 12:** The New Heaven and the New Earth
- **Vision 13:** The Symphony of the Blessed
