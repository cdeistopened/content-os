# Scivias — Book One: The Creator and Creation

## Vision 1: God Enthroned Shows Himself to Hildegard

> I saw a great mountain the color of iron, and enthroned on it One of such great glory that it blinded my sight. On each side of him there extended a soft shadow, like a wing of wondrous breadth and length. Before him, at the foot of the mountain, stood an image full of eyes on all sides, in which, because of those eyes, I could discern no human form.
>
> In front of this image stood another, a child wearing a tunic of subdued color but white shoes, upon whose head such glory descended from the One enthroned upon that mountain that I could not look at its face. But from the One who sat enthroned upon that mountain many living sparks sprang forth, which flew very sweetly around the images. Also, I perceived in this mountain many little windows, in which appeared human heads, some of subdued colors and some white.

### Interpretation

> And He Who sat upon that mountain in such great glory that it blinded my sight spoke: "The great mountain the color of iron symbolizes the strength and stability of the eternal Kingdom of God, which no fluctuation of mutability can destroy; and the One enthroned upon it of such great glory that it blinded my sight is He Who in the Kingdom of Beatitude rules the whole world with celestial divinity in the brilliance of unfading serenity, but is incomprehensible to human minds."

## Vision 2: Creation and the Fall

> Then I saw as it were a great multitude of very bright living lamps, which received fiery brilliance and acquired an unclouded splendor. And behold! A lake of great length and depth appeared, with a mouth like the mouth of a well, emitting fiery smoke with great stench, from which a loathsome cloud spread out and touched a deceitful, vein-shaped form and blew upon a white cloud that had come forth from a beautiful human form and contained within itself many and many stars, and so doing, cast out both the white cloud and the human form from that place.

### Interpretation

This vision describes the creation of the angels, the fall of Lucifer, and the creation and fall of humanity. The "great multitude of very bright living lamps" are the angels, who "received fiery brilliance" through their knowledge of God. The lake represents the abyss into which the fallen angels were cast, and the "beautiful human form" represents Adam and Eve before the Fall.

---

## Vision 3: The Universe and Its Symbolism

> After this I saw a vast instrument, round and shadowed, in the shape of an egg, small at the top, large in the middle and narrowed at the bottom; outside it, surrounding its circumference, there was bright fire with, as it were, a shadowy zone under it.

### Interpretation

This is Hildegard's famous "cosmic egg" — her vision of the universe as an egg-shaped structure. The bright fire represents the outer firmament, the shadowy zone beneath it represents the atmosphere, and humanity stands at the center of the cosmos, connected to all elements of creation. This vision has been particularly studied for its anticipation of ecological interconnectedness.

## Vision 4: Soul and Body

> Then I saw a most vast and serene splendor, flaming with many eyes, with four corners pointing toward the four directions of the world. It was manifested to me in the greatest mystery as being in the breast of a beautiful image of a human form, so that it appeared in the middle of the breast, and its height and breadth could not be measured.

### Interpretation

This vision addresses the relationship between body and soul. Hildegard teaches that the soul is not merely housed in the body but pervades it entirely, like sap in a tree — an image she connects to her concept of *viriditas* (greening power).

## Vision 5: The Synagogue

> After this I saw the image of a woman, pale from her head to her navel and black from her navel to her feet, with her feet red and surrounded by a pure white cloud. She had no eyes and held her hands in her armpits. She stood beside the altar before the eyes of God, but did not touch it.

---

## Vision 6: The Choirs of Angels

> Then I saw in the secret places of Heaven two armies of heavenly spirits who shone with great brightness. Those in one army had on their breasts as it were wings, with forms like human forms before them, on which human features appeared. Those in the second army also had on their breasts as it were wings with forms like human forms before them, in which the image of the Son of Man shone.
