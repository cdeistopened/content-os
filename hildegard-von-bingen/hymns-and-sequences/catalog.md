# Symphonia armonie celestium revelationum
## Complete Catalog of Hildegard's Musical Works

> "Symphony of the Harmony of Celestial Revelations"

Hildegard's *Symphonia* contains 77 liturgical compositions: 43 antiphons (14 votive), 18 responsories, 7 sequences, 3 hymns, 1 Kyrie, 1 Alleluia-verse, and 4 devotional songs. Additionally, the *Ordo Virtutum* morality play stands as a separate dramatic work.

The majority of these compositions are believed to date from approximately 1140–1160. Each piece was composed for specific liturgical occasions. The music is monophonic (single melodic line), characterized by soaring melodies that push the boundaries of traditional Gregorian chant, and is highly melismatic.

Sources: Riesencodex manuscript (R) at the Hessische Landesbibliothek, Wiesbaden; Dendermonde manuscript (D) at the Sint-Pieters & Paulusabdij, Dendermonde, Belgium.
