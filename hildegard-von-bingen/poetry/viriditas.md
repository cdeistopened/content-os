# Viriditas — On Greening Power

> The divine life-force that animates all creation

## What Is Viriditas?

*Viriditas* (Latin: "greenness") is Hildegard's most distinctive and original concept. It represents the divine creative force — the "greening power" — that sustains all living things. More than mere botanical metaphor, viriditas is Hildegard's term for the vitality that connects physical health, spiritual wellbeing, and cosmic order.

Its opposite is *ariditas* — dryness, barrenness, spiritual death.

---

## Collected Passages

### On the Word and Creation

> The Word is living, being, spirit, all verdant greening, all creativity. This Word manifests itself in every creature.

---

### On Viriditas as Life Force

> But life is in life. A tree flourishes from nothing else but viriditas, and even a stone is not without moisture, nor is any other creature without its power. For eternity itself is alive and not without floridity.

---

### On the Soul and Greening

> The soul is the green life-force of the flesh. For the body grows and prospers through the soul, just as the earth becomes fruitful through moisture.

> Our thinking affects our greening power. When we humans work in accord with the strivings of our soul, all our deeds turn out well.

---

### On the Holy Spirit

> The Holy Spirit is a burning and shining light that you observe in the beauty of the meadows, in the sparkle of the waters, in the shining of the sun, moon and stars. Every creature has something of this green vigor within it.

> The Holy Spirit is the "green sap" inside all of us. We are like trees, and the Holy Spirit is the capacity for juiciness, greenness, and moistness within us.

---

### On the Earth

> The earth is at the same time mother. She is mother of all that is natural, mother of all that is human. She is the mother of all, for contained in her are the seeds of all.

---

### On Sin as Dryness

> Now in the people that were meant to be green there is no more life of any kind. There is only shriveled barrenness.
>
> The winds are burdened by the utterly awful stink of evil, selfish goings-on. Thunderstorms menace. The air belches out the filthy uncleanliness of the peoples.

> A dried-up person or a dried-up culture loses the ability to create. This is the gravest sin and the deepest tragedy.

---

### On Ecological Responsibility

> The earth should not be injured! The earth must not be destroyed!

---

### On Human Creativity

> Humankind, full of all creative possibilities, is God's work. Humankind alone is called to assist God. Humankind is called to co-create. With nature's help, humankind can set into creation all that is necessary and life-sustaining.

---

### On Body and Health

> Like blood, viriditas moves through human veins and without it, humans become weak and tired and they lack spirituality. It is restored only through eating.

---

### On the Interconnection of All Things

> Everything that is in the heavens, on the earth, and under the earth is penetrated with connectedness, is penetrated with relatedness.

> God has arranged all things in the world in consideration of everything else.

---

## Viriditas in Hildegard's Hymns

The concept of viriditas appears throughout the *Symphonia*. Key musical expressions include:

- **O viridissima virga** — "O greenest branch" (Mary as the green branch of Jesse)
- **O nobilissima viriditas** — "O noblest green" (Responsory for Widows)
- **O viriditas digiti Dei** — "O greenness of God's finger" (Responsory for St. Disibod)

In each case, greenness is not decorative but theological — it represents the active, life-giving presence of God in creation.
