# Ordo Virtutum — The Order of the Virtues

> The earliest surviving morality play, composed c. 1151

## About

The *Ordo Virtutum* ("Order of the Virtues") is an allegorical morality play by Hildegard von Bingen — the earliest morality play by more than a century, and the only medieval musical drama to survive with attribution for both text and music.

Composed around 1151, it depicts the struggle between the Virtues and the Devil for a human Soul. An early version appears at the end of the Scivias, and a more developed version exists in the Riesencodex manuscript.

The play contains 82 melodies for the singing parts. Notably, the Devil has no singing part — he can only shout and growl, because he has rejected the divine harmony. This is one of Hildegard's most vivid theological statements: evil is literally discordant, unable to participate in the *symphonia* of creation.

## Dramatis Personae

- **Anima** (the Soul)
- **Diabolus** (the Devil) — speaking role only, cannot sing
- **Humilitas** (Humility) — Queen of all Virtues
- **Scientia Dei** (Knowledge of God)
- **Karitas** (Charity / Love)
- **Timor Dei** (Fear of God)
- **Obedientia** (Obedience)
- **Fides** (Faith)
- **Spes** (Hope)
- **Castitas** (Chastity)
- **Innocentia** (Innocence)
- **Contemptus Mundi** (Contempt for the World)
- **Amor Celestis** (Heavenly Love)
- **Disciplina** (Discipline)
- **Verecundia** (Modesty)
- **Misericordia** (Mercy)
- **Victoria** (Victory)
- **Discretio** (Discretion)
- **Patientia** (Patience)
- **The Patriarchs and Prophets** (chorus)

## Synopsis

### Prologue
The Patriarchs and Prophets marvel at the Virtues. The Souls cry out in longing: *"O nos peregrine sumus"* — "We are pilgrims." They acknowledge their divine origin but feel exiled in the world of sin.

### Part One: The Fall
The Soul, initially in a state of grace, encounters the Devil, who tempts her with promises of worldly pleasure. Despite the warnings of the Virtues, the Soul follows the Devil and falls.

**The Devil taunts:**
> What use is it to toil? Look at the world — it will embrace you with great honor!

**The Virtues lament:**
> O living fountain, how great was your sweetness when this Soul was set in you, for she built her wings in heaven.

### Part Two: Repentance and Return
The fallen Soul, bruised and weeping, cries out for help. The Virtues hear her and call her back:

**The Soul cries:**
> O you royal Virtues, how beautiful you appear in the highest sun, and how sweet is your dwelling — and so, woe to me that I fled from you!

**Humility responds:**
> O unhappy daughter, I will embrace you, for the great Physician has suffered grievous and bitter wounds for your sake.

### Part Three: Victory
The Virtues rally together and bind the Devil. Victory (Victoria) and Chastity (Castitas) lead the charge. The Devil is defeated not by force but by the collective harmony of the Virtues — by *symphonia*.

**Chastity declares:**
> In the mind of the Most High, O Satan, I trod upon your head, and in a virgin form I nurtured a sweet miracle, when the Son of God came into the world; therefore you are laid low with all your plunder.

**The Devil, defeated, can only shout:**
> Alas! Alas! Where shall I go? Where shall I flee? Woe is me, I cannot prevail!

### Finale
The Virtues and the restored Soul process together to God's throne, singing in harmony. The chorus calls on all to join the *symphonia*:

> Therefore, all you who see God, praise Him, for that noble Soul, whose body was a dwelling-place of sorrow, has risen to great blessedness.

## Theological Significance

The *Ordo Virtutum* expresses several of Hildegard's core beliefs:

1. **Music as divine order** — The Devil cannot sing because he has rejected God's harmony. Evil is fundamentally *discordant*.
2. **The soul's journey** — Not a linear path but a fall and return, requiring both divine grace and human will.
3. **Feminine virtue** — All the Virtues are personified as women, led by Humility as their queen.
4. **Community over isolation** — The Soul is saved not by a single heroic act but by the collective embrace of all the Virtues working together.
